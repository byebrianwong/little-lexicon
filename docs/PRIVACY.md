# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-18

This app (the "App") helps you learn advanced English vocabulary. This policy
explains what we collect, why, and your choices. It is written to be read, not
to hide behind legalese.

## What we collect

- **Account data.** When you sign up we store your email address and an optional
  display name, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth).
- **Learning data.** Your review history, spaced-repetition card state, daily
  activity, streak, XP, and settings. This is what makes scheduling and progress
  work. It is tied to your account and readable only by you.
- **Purchases.** If you subscribe, purchase and entitlement status is processed
  by the app store and RevenueCat. We store only whether your account is Pro.

We do not sell your data. We do not send your word-level performance to
advertising networks.

## How we use it

- To schedule reviews, show progress, maintain streaks and leaderboards, and
  personalize content you explicitly ask us to personalize.
- Leaderboards show only your display name, weekly XP, and rank within a small
  cohort. They never expose your individual answers.

## AI features

Optional Pro features (evaluating a sentence you write, generating a
personalized memory hook) send the specific text of that request to our AI
provider (Anthropic) through our server, only when you trigger the feature. We
do not send your broader history. These calls are rate-limited and gated to Pro.

## Your choices and rights

- **Export.** Settings, Account, Export my data returns your own data as JSON.
- **Deletion.** Settings, Account, Delete account permanently removes your
  account and all of your learning data. Shared learning content is unaffected.
- **Notifications.** Daily reminders are opt-in and local to your device.

## Data location and retention

Data is stored in our Supabase project. Learning data is retained until you
delete your account or request deletion.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the address listed on the store page.
